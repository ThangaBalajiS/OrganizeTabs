Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026
In short: Infinite Tabs Manager does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything stays on your computer.

What Data We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, browsing history or usage data from you. Period.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All data created by the extension — your saved tabs, custom groups, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your browser.

Permissions We Use

The extension requests the following browser permissions, and here's exactly why:

  • tabs — To read the titles, URLs, and favicons of your open tabs so they can be saved to your dashboard.
  • activeTab — To interact with the currently active tab when you choose to collapse selected tabs.
  • storage — To save your tabs, groups, and preferences locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The extension itself does not include any third-party analytics, tracking scripts, advertising networks, or external services that collect your data.

Chrome Web Store Analytics

We have opted into Advanced Metrics provided by the Chrome Web Store, which uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect aggregated, anonymous analytics about our store listing page — such as page views, install/uninstall counts, and general user demographics (country, language, browser version). This data is collected by Google on the Chrome Web Store page only, not within the extension itself, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

About the Share Feature

The extension previously included an optional "Share" feature that allowed you to create a shareable link by sending selected tabs to a server. This feature is currently disabled and no data is being sent anywhere. We are gathering user feedback to decide whether to bring it back in the future. If it is re-enabled, it will only send data when you explicitly choose to use it, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page with the new date. Since we don't collect your email or any contact information, we encourage you to check back here if you have concerns.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out via our feedback form.
